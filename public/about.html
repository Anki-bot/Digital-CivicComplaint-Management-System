About JanSeva 🏛️

Mission: every civic issue visible, SLA-bound and closable with proof.

What we do

JanSeva connects citizens to 6 city departments with map pins, photo proof, priority SLAs (24–168h), upvotes, comments, officer remarks, receipts and star ratings — all transparent.

SLA promise

Urgent 24h • High 48h • Medium 96h • Low 168h. Overdue cases auto-highlight for admins. Resolution needs an official remark + timeline entry.

How to get fastest action

1) Exact landmark + ward 2) Clear daylight photo 3) Right category 4) Urgent only for genuine emergencies 5) Ask neighbours to upvote.
