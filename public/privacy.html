Privacy Policy 🔒

JanSeva stores your account (bcrypt-hashed password — never plaintext), complaints, photos and remarks in the portal's SQLite database to operate grievance redressal. Anonymous mode hides your name publicly (admins still see your email for follow-up). Photos are limited to 2MB images. You can edit your profile, delete Pending complaints, and request account removal via the helpline (1800-111-000). Demo logins are public test accounts — use a fresh registration for personal data.
